# 07 · The care guide: a web page inside the app

The page is bundled in the app and loads without a network. Its elements carry HTML ids (web_name, web_submit, …).

1. Open Mossgrove and sign in with tester@mossgrove.test and Mossgrove123.
   Expect: The shop opens and the screen shows "Stoneware Mug".
2. Open the Lab tab and tap "Care guide".
   Expect: The screen shows "Care guide loaded".
3. Type "Sam" in the page's "Your name" field.
4. Choose "Every week" in the page's "Remind me" list.
5. Tick "Email me the guide".
6. Tap Save on the page.
   Expect: The page shows "Saved, Sam. We will remind you every week."
7. Tap "Jump to washing".
   Expect: The page shows "Washing".
